Support

Need help, found a bug, or a level seems wrong? Email rahnemon2011@gmail.com. Inside a level you can also open the ⋮ menu and tap Report issue / Feedback: it fills in the level for you.

Frequently asked questions

An arrow won't move. An arrow only slides if its path to the edge of the board is clear. Free the arrows in front of it first. The light bulb shows one arrow that can move.

How do I remove ads? Buy "Remove ads" once in the You tab. It works forever on your account. On a new phone, tap Restore purchase.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your phone after every level. With Google Play Cloud Save (Android) or iCloud (iPhone) it also moves to a new phone.

Can I change ad privacy choices? Yes, in You → About Arrowick. See also our privacy policy.
