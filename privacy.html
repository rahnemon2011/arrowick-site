Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Arrowick is an arrow puzzle game for Android and iPhone, made by Hadi Mohammadi (Berlin, Germany). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. Questions: rahnemon2011@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • You can play without an account. We don't run our own servers, analytics or tracking.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your device.
  • The free app shows a few ads from Google AdMob. Google may use some device data to show and measure them.
  • We never sell your personal data.

2. Information stored on your device

The app saves your progress (levels cleared, marks earned, daily levels played) and your settings on your device so you can continue where you left off. We cannot see this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. Your phone's own backup (Google or iCloud) may keep a copy, which you control in your phone settings.

3. Cloud saves (optional)

If you connect Google Play Games (Android), or are signed in to iCloud (iPhone), your progress can be saved there so you can move it to a new device. That data is handled by Google or Apple under their privacy policies.

4. Ads

The free app shows occasional short ads from Google AdMob between levels, never while you are solving a level. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), the app asks for your consent first. You can change your choice at any time in You → About Arrowick → Ad privacy options, and you can reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone settings. The iPhone app does not ask to track you and does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) for tracking. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

5. Purchases

You can buy "Remove ads" once. Payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or billing details; we only receive a confirmation that the purchase was made.

6. Feedback and notifications

If you send us feedback or a bug report, it is sent by email from your own email app, and we only use it to answer you and fix problems. Daily reminders are created on your device and don't use our servers. You can turn them off at any time.

7. Children

Arrowick is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR), you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or withdraw your consent. We usually hold no data about you apart from emails you send us. Contact rahnemon2011@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
